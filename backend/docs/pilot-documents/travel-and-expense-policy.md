# Business Travel and Expense Reimbursement Policy

## 1. Overview and Scope
This policy establishes standard guidelines and spending thresholds for all full-time and contract employees traveling on behalf of the company. The objective is to ensure employees travel safely and comfortably while maintaining fiscal responsibility.

All business travel must receive pre-authorization from the employee's direct manager at least 14 business days prior to departure for domestic travel, and 30 business days for international travel.

## 2. Flight Booking and Class of Service
- **Domestic Flights (Under 6 Hours)**: Employees must book Economy or Main Cabin seating through the corporate travel booking portal.
- **Long-Haul Flights (Over 6 Consecutive Hours)**: Employees are authorized to book Premium Economy. Business Class bookings require written approval from a Vice President or C-level executive.
- **Seat Selection**: Standard seat selection fees are reimbursable. Paid upgrades to extra legroom or first class are personal expenses unless pre-approved for medical accommodations.
- **Baggage Fees**: One checked baggage fee is reimbursable for domestic trips. For international trips lasting more than 5 calendar days, up to two checked bags are permitted.

## 3. Lodging and Hotel Guidelines
Employees should select standard rooms at reputable business hotels located within reasonable proximity to client sites or event venues.
The maximum allowable nightly room rates (excluding local taxes and mandatory resort fees) are:
- **Major Metropolitan Tier-1 Cities** (e.g., New York, San Francisco, London, Tokyo): Up to $275 per night.
- **Standard Metropolitan Cities**: Up to $195 per night.
- **Other Domestic Locations**: Up to $150 per night.

Room service, pay-per-view movies, in-room minibar items, and spa services are strictly personal expenses and will not be reimbursed.

## 4. Meals and Per Diem Allowances
The company operates on a per diem reimbursement model for daily meals and incidentals during travel. Receipts are not required for individual meals if claiming within the per diem cap:
- **Domestic Daily Meal Allowance**: $75 per day (Breakfast: $15, Lunch: $25, Dinner: $35).
- **International Daily Meal Allowance**: $120 per day (Breakfast: $25, Lunch: $40, Dinner: $55).
- **First and Last Day of Travel**: Reimbursed at 75% of the applicable daily per diem rate.
- **Alcohol**: Alcoholic beverages are not reimbursable unless part of an authorized client entertainment dinner attended by at least one external client representative.

## 5. Ground Transportation
- **Rideshare and Taxis**: Standard tier rideshares (UberX, Lyft standard) or metered taxis are approved for transportation between airports, hotels, and business meetings. Premium tiers (Uber Black, Lyft Lux) are non-reimbursable.
- **Rental Cars**: Compact or mid-size rental cars are approved when cost-effective compared to taxis. Collision Damage Waiver (CDW) insurance should be accepted under corporate insurance.
- **Personal Vehicle Mileage**: When an employee drives a personal vehicle for authorized business travel, mileage is reimbursed at $0.67 per mile, covering fuel, maintenance, and insurance.

## 6. Expense Submission and Receipt Deadlines
All expense reports must be submitted via the corporate Finance Portal within 30 calendar days of the completion of the business trip.
- Itemized receipts are required for all individual expenses exceeding $25.
- Credit card summary slips that do not display itemized items are insufficient for audit compliance.
- Expenses submitted after 60 calendar days will be rejected and considered non-reimbursable.
