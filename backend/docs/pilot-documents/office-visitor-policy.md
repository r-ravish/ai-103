# Physical Office Visitor and Facility Security Policy

## Visitor Registration Protocol
All non-employee guests, contractors, vendors, and interview candidates visiting company corporate offices must register at the reception lobby kiosk upon arrival.
- Visitors must present a valid government-issued photo ID.
- Visitors must sign the digital Visitor Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA).
- A printed Visitor Badge must be visibly worn on outer clothing at all times while on company premises.

## Host Escort Responsibilities
Guests must be accompanied by an authorized employee host at all times. Visitors may never be left unattended in working zones, conference rooms, or communal kitchens. When the meeting concludes, the employee host is responsible for escorting the guest back to the reception lobby to return their badge and check out.

## Restricted Access Zones
Certain facility zones are strictly designated as Restricted Access and are completely off-limits to external visitors under all circumstances:
- Server and network infrastructure rooms
- Hardware engineering and prototype testing labs
- Physical archival file storage rooms

Access to restricted zones requires biometric multi-factor authentication and prior written authorization from the Chief Information Security Officer (CISO). Photography and audio/video recording are strictly forbidden throughout all office facilities.
