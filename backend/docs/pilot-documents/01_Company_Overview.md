## Who We Are
NovaWorks Technologies is a mid-sized enterprise software company founded in **2011** in Bengaluru, India. We build cloud-based knowledge management, IT service, and HR automation tools used by mid-to-large organizations across Asia-Pacific, Europe, and North America.

## Founding Story
NovaWorks was founded by **Ananya Rao** and **Vikram Sethi**, two engineers who previously worked together at a large systems-integration firm. They started the company after noticing that most of the support tickets and internal help-desk queries they saw were answers that already existed somewhere in company documentation — just impossible to find quickly. That observation is still the company's founding thesis today: **information exists, findability is the problem.**
