# Leave & Attendance Policy — NovaWorks Technologies

## Document Info
- **Document ID:** POL-HR-003
- **Owner:** People Team (HR Operations)
- **Last Reviewed:** September 2026
- **Applies to:** All full-time employees (pro-rated for mid-year joiners)

## 1. Leave Types & Annual Entitlement

| Leave Type | Days/Year | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Casual Leave (CL) | 8 | For short personal matters; can be taken with 1 day's notice |
| Sick Leave (SL) | 10 | Self-certified up to 2 consecutive days; medical certificate required beyond that |
| Earned/Privilege Leave (EL) | 18 | Accrues at 1.5 days/month; used for planned vacations |
| Maternity Leave | 182 (26 weeks) | As per statutory requirement; full pay |
| Paternity Leave | 15 | Full pay, to be taken within 6 months of child's birth |
| Bereavement Leave | 5 | Immediate family; full pay |
| Public Holidays | 10–12 | Varies by office location; calendar published every January |

Total combined CL + SL + EL: **36 days/year** for a full-time employee, pro-rated for new joiners based on month of joining.

## 2. How to Apply
1. Log in to **NovaWorks Desk — HR Module**.
2. Select "Apply for Leave," choose leave type, dates, and reason.
3. Your request routes automatically to your reporting manager for approval.
4. For Sick Leave beyond 2 days, upload a medical certificate in the same request.
5. You'll receive a status notification (approved/rejected/pending) within 1 business day.

**Unplanned/emergency leave:** Notify your manager directly (call, Slack, or email) before your normal start time, then regularize it in the HR system within 2 business days.

## 3. Notice Period for Planned Leave
- **1–2 days CL:** Apply at least 1 day in advance.
- **3+ days EL:** Apply at least 7 days in advance to allow manager workload planning.
- **Leave during critical project milestones:** Discuss with your manager even if formal notice requirements are met.

## 4. Carry-Forward Rules
- **Earned Leave (EL):** Up to **15 days** can be carried forward to the next calendar year. Anything beyond 15 days lapses on December 31, unless locally mandated otherwise.
- **Casual Leave (CL):** Does not carry forward; unused CL lapses at year-end.
- **Sick Leave (SL):** Does not carry forward; unused SL lapses at year-end.
- **Encashment:** Employees may encash up to 10 accumulated EL days per year, subject to Finance approval, via the HR portal.

## 5. Leave Without Pay (LWP)
If an employee exhausts all leave balances, they may request Leave Without Pay, subject to manager and HR approval. LWP beyond 30 continuous days requires Chief People Officer sign-off and may affect benefits continuity.

## 6. Attendance Expectations
- Attendance is tracked via the HR portal's daily check-in (for office-based roles) or a self-reported timesheet (for remote/hybrid roles).
- Repeated unplanned absences (3+ instances in a rolling 30-day window without regularization) trigger an automatic HR check-in — this is a conversation, not an automatic disciplinary step.

## Common Questions
- **"How many casual leaves do I have left?"** → Check the "My Leave Balance" widget in NovaWorks Desk — HR Module; this is a live lookup, not something answerable from this document alone (route to an HR data tool/action, not just retrieval).
- **"Can I carry forward my casual leave?"** → No, only Earned Leave carries forward (up to 15 days).
- **"What if I'm sick for a week?"** → First 2 days self-certified; a medical certificate is required for day 3 onward.

*Note for knowledge agent design: leave **policy rules** are answerable from this document; a specific employee's **current leave balance** requires a live data/tool lookup (e.g., via the custom MCP server), not document retrieval.*
