# Proprietary Information and Intellectual Property Agreement

## 1. Ownership of Inventions and Works
All inventions, technological improvements, software source code, designs, algorithms, and copyrighted materials created, authored, or conceived by an employee during their period of employment—whether created during normal working hours or using company resources—shall be the sole and exclusive property of the company ("Work Made for Hire").

Employees agree to assign, and hereby irrevocably assign, all worldwide rights, titles, and interests in such inventions to the company without entitlement to additional royalties.

## 2. Prior Inventions and Carve-Out Disclosures
If an employee holds rights to inventions, patents, or open-source software libraries created prior to joining the company, they must formally declare them on **Exhibit IP-101 (Prior Inventions Schedule)** within 14 calendar days of their start date.

Any inventions not explicitly disclosed on Exhibit IP-101 that relate directly to the company's business or products shall be presumed to have been created during employment.

## 3. Open Source Software Contributions
The company encourages responsible participation in the global developer community subject to the following rules:
- **Personal Projects**: Contributing to personal open-source projects on personal time and personal devices is permitted, provided no proprietary code, customer data, or confidential algorithms are utilized.
- **Corporate Open Source Contributions**: Contributing to third-party open source projects using company email, time, or equipment requires prior written review and approval from the **Open Source Review Board (OSRB)** via email to `osrb@company.internal`.
- **Approved Permissive Licenses**: Permitted licenses for inbound code consumption include MIT, Apache 2.0, and BSD-3-Clause. Copyleft licenses (GPLv2, GPLv3, AGPL) are strictly prohibited in commercial product distributions without legal team sign-off.
