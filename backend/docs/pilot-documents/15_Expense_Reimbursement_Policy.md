# Expense & Reimbursement Policy — NovaWorks Technologies

## Document Info
- **Document ID:** POL-FIN-001
- **Owner:** Finance
- **Last Reviewed:** September 2026
- **Applies to:** All employees

## 1. What's Claimable
| Category | Claimable | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Client meals/entertainment | Yes | Reasonable limits; itemized receipt required |
| Travel (flights, trains, cabs) | Yes | Economy class for domestic; Premium Economy for international flights over 6 hours |
| Hotel/accommodation | Yes | Per approved per-diem caps by city tier |
| Internet/phone (remote employees) | Yes | Fixed monthly allowance, see Compensation & Benefits Overview |
| Professional certifications/courses | Yes, with pre-approval | Requires manager + L&D sign-off before enrolling |
| Personal commute (regular office days) | No | Not reimbursable except for approved late-night work (post 9 PM) cab claims |
| Alcohol | Limited | Only as part of client entertainment, within reasonable limits, itemized separately |
| Personal purchases unrelated to work | No | — |

## 2. Approval Workflow
1. Employee submits an expense claim via **NovaWorks Desk — Finance Module**, attaching itemized receipts and selecting a cost category.
2. Claims under a threshold (e.g., local equivalent of $100) auto-route to direct manager for one-step approval.
3. Claims above the threshold, or involving travel/client entertainment, require manager approval **and** a Finance Business Partner review.
4. Approved claims are processed in the next payroll/reimbursement cycle (see Section 3).
5. Rejected claims include a reason and can be resubmitted with corrections/additional documentation.

## 3. Submission Deadlines & Payout
- Expenses must be submitted **within 30 days** of the expense date; claims submitted later require a written justification and manager override approval.
- Approved reimbursements are paid out within **5–7 business days** after final approval, via the same payroll channel as salary.
- Corporate card holders (Sales/Travel-heavy roles) must reconcile statements monthly; unreconciled corporate card spend beyond 45 days may be deducted from payroll.

## 4. Travel Policy
- **Booking:** All business travel must be booked through the approved corporate travel portal (linked in NovaWorks Desk) to ensure policy compliance and negotiated rates.
- **Advance approval:** Trips must be approved by your manager before booking; international travel additionally requires department head sign-off.
- **Class of travel:** Economy for domestic and short-haul international; Premium Economy permitted for international flights over 6 hours; Business class only for Director-level and above or with CFO exception.
- **Per diem:** Daily meal/incidental allowance varies by city tier (published in the travel portal), covering meals not otherwise claimed separately.
- **Personal travel combined with business trips:** Permitted, but the employee bears any cost difference and must clearly separate personal-day expenses from business claims.

## 5. Corporate Cards
Issued to roles with frequent travel/client spend (Sales, Customer Success, Executive team). Cardholders must submit itemized reconciliation monthly; misuse (personal spend without reimbursement) is treated as a Code of Conduct violation.

## Common Questions
- **"What can I claim as a business expense?"** → See the table in Section 1 — travel, client meals, approved training, and remote work allowances are claimable; personal commute and unrelated purchases are not.
- **"How long do I have to submit an expense?"** → 30 days from the expense date.
- **"When will I get reimbursed?"** → 5–7 business days after final approval.
- **"Can I fly business class?"** → Only Director-level and above, or with a CFO-approved exception.

*This document should ground claimability, approval workflow, deadline, and travel-class questions. Specific claim statuses require a live lookup via the Finance Module, not this document.*
