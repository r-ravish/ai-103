# Compensation & Benefits Overview — NovaWorks Technologies

## Document Info
- **Document ID:** POL-HR-004
- **Owner:** People Team / Finance (Payroll)
- **Last Reviewed:** September 2026
- **Applies to:** All full-time employees
- **Note:** This is a high-level, non-confidential overview. Individual salary details are never disclosed via the knowledge assistant.

## 1. Payroll Cycle
- **Pay frequency:** Monthly.
- **Pay date:** Last working day of the month; if it falls on a weekend/holiday, disbursed on the preceding business day.
- **Payslip access:** Available in NovaWorks Desk — HR Module under "Payroll" by the 1st of the following month.
- **Currency:** Local currency of employment location (INR, USD, EUR, SGD).

## 2. Compensation Structure (General)
A typical total compensation package consists of:
- **Base salary** — fixed monthly component.
- **Variable/performance bonus** — annual, tied to company and individual performance (see Section 4).
- **Retention/joining bonus** — role-dependent, disbursed per offer letter terms, often with a payback clause if the employee exits within 12 months.
- **Statutory benefits** — provident fund/retirement contributions, gratuity (India), 401(k) matching (US), or local equivalents.

## 3. Insurance & Health Benefits
| Benefit | Coverage |
|---|---|
| Group Medical Insurance | Employee + spouse + up to 2 children, sum insured varies by grade/location |
| Group Term Life Insurance | 3x annual base salary, fully employer-paid |
| Accidental Disability Cover | Included with life insurance |
| Annual Health Checkup | One free comprehensive checkup per year at empaneled hospitals/clinics |

Enrollment happens during onboarding; changes (adding a dependent, opting for higher coverage) can be made during the annual open enrollment window or upon a qualifying life event.

## 4. Bonus Structure (High-Level)
- **Annual Performance Bonus:** Target of 10–20% of base salary depending on role/level, paid out based on a combination of company performance multiplier and individual appraisal rating (see Performance Review & Appraisal Policy).
- **Spot Bonuses:** Managers can nominate employees for smaller discretionary bonuses for exceptional contributions, approved by department head.
- **Sales Commission (Sales roles only):** Governed by a separate Sales Incentive Plan document, not covered here.

## 5. Reimbursements
Reimbursable expenses (subject to receipts and policy limits):
- Internet/phone allowance for remote employees (monthly cap, region-dependent).
- Approved travel and client-related expenses (see Expense & Reimbursement Policy for full details).
- Professional certification/training costs, pre-approved by manager and L&D.
- Relocation expenses for approved inter-office transfers.

**How to claim:** Submit via NovaWorks Desk — Finance Module → "Reimbursement Request" with receipts attached. Standard processing time is 5–7 business days after manager approval.

## 6. Other Benefits
- **Wellness stipend:** Annual allowance for gym/wellness activities.
- **Learning stipend:** Annual allowance for courses, books, or conference attendance, subject to L&D approval.
- **Employee referral bonus:** Paid out when a referred candidate completes 90 days of employment.

## Common Questions
- **"When do I get paid?"** → Last working day of the month.
- **"What insurance am I covered under?"** → Group Medical + Group Term Life, details in Section 3; specific plan documents are shared during onboarding.
- **"How do I claim a reimbursement?"** → Submit via NovaWorks Desk — Finance Module; see Section 5.

*Individual compensation figures, exact bonus payouts, and personal payslip data are confidential and must never be surfaced through the general-purpose knowledge assistant — route those requests to the HR self-service portal or an authenticated, permission-scoped tool call.*
