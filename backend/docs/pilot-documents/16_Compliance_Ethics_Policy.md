# Compliance & Ethics Policy — NovaWorks Technologies

## Document Info
- **Document ID:** POL-LEGAL-001
- **Owner:** General Counsel / Compliance
- **Last Reviewed:** September 2026
- **Applies to:** All employees, contractors, and board members

## 1. Purpose
This policy sets baseline ethical and regulatory compliance standards for how NovaWorks employees conduct business, covering anti-bribery, conflicts of interest, and mechanisms to raise concerns without fear of retaliation.

## 2. Anti-Bribery & Anti-Corruption
- Employees must never offer, give, solicit, or accept bribes, kickbacks, or improper payments to/from customers, vendors, government officials, or partners, in any country of operation.
- **Gifts & hospitality:** Gifts or hospitality above INR 5,000 (or local equivalent) from/to a vendor or client must be declined or reported to Finance/Compliance; token, occasional gifts (branded merchandise, modest meals) are generally acceptable.
- **Government officials:** Any interaction involving payments, gifts, or favors to government officials requires prior Legal review — this is a zero-tolerance, high-risk area globally (covered by frameworks like the FCPA and India's Prevention of Corruption Act).
- **Facilitation payments:** Prohibited in all circumstances, even where locally customary.
- Third parties (agents, resellers, consultants) acting on NovaWorks' behalf must be vetted and contractually bound to the same anti-bribery standards.

## 3. Conflict of Interest
A conflict of interest exists when personal interests (financial, familial, or otherwise) could influence — or appear to influence — an employee's business decisions. Common examples requiring disclosure:
- Financial stake in a vendor, competitor, or client.
- A close family member employed by or owning a vendor/competitor.
- Outside employment or consulting that overlaps with NovaWorks' business.
- Involvement in hiring, procurement, or vendor decisions involving a personal connection.

**Disclosure process:** Complete a Conflict of Interest declaration in NovaWorks Desk — Compliance Module upon joining, and update it whenever a new conflict arises. Compliance reviews disclosures and may require recusal from specific decisions.

## 4. Whistleblower Policy
- Employees who observe suspected fraud, bribery, regulatory violations, financial misreporting, or serious ethical breaches are encouraged — and expected — to report them.
- **Reporting channels:**
  1. Direct to manager (if not implicated).
  2. Compliance team via compliance@novaworks-example.com.
  3. **Anonymous Ethics Hotline** (third-party operated, available 24/7) via NovaWorks Desk → "Report a Concern."
- **Protection:** NovaWorks prohibits retaliation against any employee who reports in good faith, even if the concern is ultimately not substantiated. Retaliation is itself a terminable violation.
- **Investigation:** Compliance and, where necessary, external counsel investigate substantiated reports; findings are reported to the Audit Committee of the Board for significant matters.

## 5. Regulatory Compliance Basics
- **Data protection:** Compliance with GDPR (EU operations), India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act), and equivalent regional laws — see the Information Security & Data Protection Policy for operational detail.
- **Export controls & sanctions:** Employees must not engage in business with sanctioned entities/countries; Sales and Legal jointly screen new customer contracts.
- **Anti-money laundering (AML):** Finance conducts vendor/customer due diligence for large or unusual transactions.
- **Labor law:** Local employment law (working hours, leave entitlements, termination rules) governs and supersedes internal policy where there's a conflict — HR and Legal jointly maintain region-specific addenda.

## 6. Mandatory Training & Acknowledgment
All employees complete Compliance & Ethics training during onboarding and annually thereafter; completion is required to retain system access, and the training explicitly covers real anonymized case examples from the whistleblower program.

## Common Questions
- **"Can I accept a gift from a vendor?"** → Only if under the INR 5,000 (or local equivalent) threshold and not tied to a pending decision; otherwise decline or report to Compliance.
- **"How do I report suspected fraud?"** → Use the anonymous Ethics Hotline via NovaWorks Desk, or email compliance@novaworks-example.com.
- **"What if I have a family member at a vendor company?"** → Disclose it via the Conflict of Interest declaration in the Compliance Module; you'll likely be recused from related decisions.
- **"Will I get in trouble for reporting something that turns out to be nothing?"** → No — good-faith reports are protected regardless of outcome.
