EMPLOYEE PROBATION AND PERFORMANCE REVIEW GUIDELINES

1. Probationary Period Framework
All newly appointed regular full-time and part-time staff members are subject to an introductory probationary evaluation period of ninety (90) calendar days commencing on their initial employment start date.
The probationary timeframe provides an opportunity for both the employee and the department supervisor to assess work aptitude, job competencies, cultural integration, and general suitability for the permanent role.

2. Interim 45-Day Check-in
Supervisors are mandated to perform a formal mid-probation milestone review on or about the 45th calendar day of tenure.
This evaluation evaluates progress against established 30-60-90 day goal charters, highlights strengths demonstrated, and delineates any remedial skills coaching required prior to confirmation.

3. Probation Completion and Extension
Upon reaching the 90-day threshold, the manager must submit a Probation Completion Determination form to the Human Resources Operations department.
Three potential outcomes exist:
- Regular Confirmation: Employment status converts to regular permanent status.
- Extension of Probation: In exceptional circumstances where additional assessment time is warranted, probation may be extended for a single period not exceeding sixty (60) calendar days with written agreement from HR.
- Employment Separation: If performance standards are demonstrably unmet despite guidance, employment will be terminated in accordance with the offer letter terms.

4. Annual Performance Evaluation Cycle
Permanent employees participate in the enterprise-wide annual performance assessment cadence:
- Self-Evaluations Open: November 1st of each calendar year.
- Manager Appraisals Complete: December 15th.
- Compensation & Merit Adjustments Effective: February 1st of the following year.

Performance is rated on a 5-point calibration scale:
Rating 1 - Unsatisfactory
Rating 2 - Needs Development
Rating 3 - Meets Expectations
Rating 4 - Exceeds Expectations
Rating 5 - Outstanding Leader

Employees who receive an overall calibration rating of 1 are placed on a formal 30-to-60 day Performance Improvement Plan (PIP). Successful PIP graduation requires meeting 100% of defined weekly remedial objectives.
