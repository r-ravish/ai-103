# Code of Conduct & Anti-Harassment Policy — NovaWorks Technologies

## Document Info
- **Document ID:** POL-HR-006
- **Owner:** People Team / Legal & Compliance
- **Last Reviewed:** September 2026
- **Applies to:** All employees, contractors, interns, and vendors on-site

## 1. Purpose
NovaWorks is committed to providing a workplace free from harassment, discrimination, and retaliation, in compliance with applicable local law, including India's **Prevention of Sexual Harassment (POSH) Act, 2013**, and equivalent frameworks in the US, EU, and Singapore.

## 2. Workplace Behavior Standards
Expected of every employee:
- Treat all colleagues, clients, vendors, and candidates with dignity and respect.
- No discrimination based on gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, race, religion, caste, disability, age, or marital status.
- No unwelcome physical contact, sexually suggestive remarks, display of offensive material, or persistent unwanted attention.
- No abuse of power dynamics — supervisors must not use authority to solicit favors or create a hostile environment.
- Respectful conduct extends to virtual settings: video calls, Slack, email, and social media referencing colleagues.

## 3. What Constitutes Harassment
Includes, but is not limited to:
- Verbal: slurs, jokes, or comments targeting a protected characteristic.
- Physical: unwanted touching, blocking movement, physical intimidation.
- Visual: displaying offensive images, gestures, or sharing inappropriate content.
- Digital: inappropriate messages, unwanted contact outside work hours, sharing private images without consent.
- Quid pro quo: conditioning job benefits (promotion, raise, project assignment) on personal favors.

## 4. Reporting Mechanism
Employees can report a concern through any of the following channels:
1. **Direct to manager** (if the concern doesn't involve the manager).
2. **HR Business Partner** — via email or a private meeting request.
3. **Internal Complaints Committee (ICC)** — the statutory body for POSH complaints in India; equivalent regional committees exist for other geographies.
4. **Anonymous Ethics Hotline** — accessible via NovaWorks Desk — HR Module → "Report a Concern," available 24/7, third-party operated for confidentiality.

**No retaliation:** Any employee who reports in good faith, or participates in an investigation, is protected from retaliation. Retaliation is itself a serious violation subject to disciplinary action.

## 5. Grievance / Investigation Process
1. **Acknowledgment:** Complaint acknowledged within 2 business days.
2. **Investigation:** Conducted by the ICC (or regional equivalent) within **90 days**, as mandated by POSH regulations for India-based complaints; other regions follow local statutory timelines.
3. **Confidentiality:** Details are shared only with parties directly involved in the investigation, on a need-to-know basis.
4. **Outcome:** Findings are communicated to both parties. Substantiated complaints result in disciplinary action ranging from a written warning to termination, depending on severity.
5. **Appeal:** Either party may appeal the outcome to the Chief People Officer within 15 days of the decision.

## 6. Disciplinary Consequences
| Severity | Example | Typical Action |
|---|---|---|
| Minor | Inappropriate joke, first offense | Verbal warning + mandatory training |
| Moderate | Repeated unwelcome comments, hostile behavior | Written warning, PIP, possible role/team change |
| Severe | Physical harassment, quid pro quo, retaliation | Immediate termination, possible legal referral |

## 7. Mandatory Training
All employees complete Code of Conduct and Anti-Harassment training during onboarding (Day 1) and annually thereafter via the L&D portal. Completion is tracked and required for continued system access.

## Common Questions
- **"How do I report harassment?"** → Use the anonymous Ethics Hotline via NovaWorks Desk, or contact your HR Business Partner directly.
- **"Will reporting affect my job?"** → No — retaliation against a good-faith reporter is itself a policy violation.
- **"How long does an investigation take?"** → Up to 90 days for POSH-governed complaints in India; other regions follow local timelines.
