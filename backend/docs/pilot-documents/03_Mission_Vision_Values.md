# Mission, Vision & Values — NovaWorks Technologies

## Document Info
- **Document ID:** POL-COMP-003
- **Owner:** Corporate Communications
- **Last Reviewed:** September 2026
- **Audience:** All employees, new hires

## Mission
To make every piece of organizational knowledge findable, trustworthy, and actionable — so employees spend their time solving problems, not searching for answers.

## Vision
A world where every company, regardless of size, has an AI-powered knowledge layer that knows what it knows, knows what it doesn't, and always tells employees the truth about both.

## Core Values

### 1. Findability Over Volume
We don't measure success by how much content exists — we measure it by how fast someone finds the right answer. A single accurate, well-tagged document beats a hundred stale ones.

### 2. Cite Everything
Every claim we make — in our product and internally — should be traceable to a source. Unverified answers are worse than no answer.

### 3. Say "I Don't Know" Proudly
Confidently wrong is our biggest risk. We reward employees and systems that flag uncertainty rather than guess.

### 4. Build for the Person Who Joined Yesterday
Every process, document, and tool should be usable by someone with zero tribal knowledge. If only a 5-year veteran can use it, it's broken.

### 5. Respect the Boundary
Permission-aware by default. Just because information exists doesn't mean everyone should see it. We design access controls before we design convenience.

## How Values Show Up Day to Day
- Engineering reviews require linking design decisions to a documented rationale (supports "Cite Everything").
- New hires get a formal 30-day feedback checkpoint (supports "Build for the Person Who Joined Yesterday").
- Any AI or automation feature shipped must include a documented confidence/escalation behavior (supports "Say 'I Don't Know' Proudly").
- Data access requests go through role-based approval, not ad-hoc sharing (supports "Respect the Boundary").

## Culture Statement
NovaWorks is a low-hierarchy, high-documentation culture. We prefer async, written communication over meetings, and we expect every team to keep their own knowledge base current — the same standard we sell to customers, we hold ourselves to internally.
