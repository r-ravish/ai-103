# Employee Handbook — NovaWorks Technologies

## Document Info
- **Document ID:** POL-HR-001
- **Owner:** People Team (HR)
- **Last Reviewed:** September 2026
- **Applies to:** All full-time and contract employees

## 1. Purpose
This handbook is the umbrella reference for day-to-day conduct, work expectations, and general employment terms at NovaWorks. Where a specific policy document (e.g., Leave Policy, IT Acceptable Use Policy) exists, that document governs; this handbook covers everything not addressed elsewhere.

## 2. Working Hours
- **Standard hours:** 9:30 AM – 6:30 PM, local office time, Monday–Friday.
- **Core collaboration hours:** 11:00 AM – 4:00 PM local time — employees are expected to be reachable during this window regardless of flexible scheduling.
- **Flexible start:** Employees may start any time between 8:00 AM and 10:30 AM as long as they complete 8 working hours.
- **Overtime:** Non-exempt roles must get manager pre-approval for overtime; exempt roles are not eligible for overtime pay.

## 3. Dress Code
- **Office days:** Business casual. No formal dress code beyond avoiding beachwear, gym wear, or offensive graphics.
- **Client-facing roles:** Business formal on days with in-person or video client meetings.
- **Remote days:** No dress code enforced, but employees on video calls should wear attire appropriate for a professional setting.

## 4. Code of Conduct
- Treat colleagues, clients, and vendors with respect regardless of role, seniority, or background.
- Conflicts of interest (e.g., financial stake in a vendor or competitor) must be disclosed to your manager and HR.
- Gifts from vendors/clients above INR 5,000 (or local equivalent) must be declined or reported to Finance.
- Use of company assets (laptops, software licenses, credentials) is for business purposes; incidental personal use is permitted within reason.
- Confidential company and customer information must never be shared outside the organization, including on personal social media.

## 5. Attendance & Leave (Summary)
Full details live in the dedicated Leave & Attendance Policy. In brief:
- Notify your manager and mark leave in the HR system in advance for planned absences.
- For unplanned/sick leave, notify your manager before your normal start time.
- Excessive unplanned absences trigger a check-in conversation with your manager and HR, not automatic disciplinary action.

## 6. Communication Norms
- Default to asynchronous, written communication (Slack/email/tickets) over meetings.
- Response-time expectation: same business day for Slack, within 24 business hours for email.
- Meetings should have a written agenda; if there's no agenda, it's acceptable to ask for one before attending.

## 7. Anti-Harassment & Grievance
NovaWorks has zero tolerance for harassment, discrimination, or retaliation. Employees can report concerns to their manager, HR, or anonymously via the Ethics Hotline (see Code of Conduct & Anti-Harassment Policy for the full process). No employee will face retaliation for a good-faith report.

## 8. Disciplinary Process
Minor issues follow a verbal → written warning → performance improvement plan escalation path. Serious violations (harassment, fraud, data breaches) may result in immediate termination, handled jointly by HR and Legal.

## 9. Amendments
This handbook is reviewed annually by the People Team. Material changes are communicated via company-wide email and require acknowledgment in the HR system within 14 days.

*This document should ground general conduct, working hours, and dress code questions. For leave balances, benefits, or exit procedures, route to their dedicated policy documents.*
