# New Hire Onboarding Guide — NovaWorks Technologies

## Document Info
- **Document ID:** POL-HR-002
- **Owner:** People Team — Talent Acquisition & L&D
- **Last Reviewed:** September 2026
- **Applies to:** All new full-time employees and interns

## Before Day 1
- HR sends an offer confirmation email with your start date, reporting manager, and office location.
- IT provisions your company email account and laptop shipment (remote hires) or desk setup (office hires) 3 business days before start date.
- You'll receive login credentials for the HR portal (**NovaWorks Desk — HR module**) to complete pre-joining paperwork.

## First-Week Checklist

| Day | Activity |
|---|---|
| Day 1 | HR orientation session, laptop/equipment handover, IT account activation, buddy assignment |
| Day 1 | Complete mandatory compliance training: Code of Conduct, Information Security basics |
| Day 2 | Meet your manager for role expectations and 30-60-90 day plan |
| Day 2–3 | Tool setup: Slack, email, VPN, ticketing system (NovaWorks Desk), Jira/Confluence access |
| Day 3 | Meet your assigned onboarding buddy for informal Q&A and team introductions |
| Day 4 | Department-specific onboarding session (Engineering, Sales, HR, Finance, or Marketing track) |
| Day 5 | First 1:1 check-in with manager; confirm all system access is working |

## Tools You'll Need Access To
- **Email & Calendar** — Microsoft 365
- **Chat** — Slack (#general, #your-team, #random channels auto-joined)
- **Ticketing/Helpdesk** — NovaWorks Desk (for IT and HR requests)
- **Docs/Wiki** — Confluence
- **Code repos** (Engineering only) — GitHub, provisioned by your manager
- **VPN** — required for accessing internal systems remotely; request via IT ticket if not auto-provisioned

## Key Contacts
| Need | Contact |
|---|---|
| IT/laptop/access issues | helpdesk@novaworks-example.com or raise a ticket in NovaWorks Desk |
| Payroll/benefits questions | hr-ops@novaworks-example.com |
| Onboarding buddy | Assigned by your manager, announced on Day 1 |
| General HR questions | Your regional HR Business Partner (listed in the HR portal) |

## Orientation Schedule (Week 1 Overview)
- **Day 1, 10:00 AM:** Welcome session with People Team (company overview, values, benefits walkthrough)
- **Day 1, 2:00 PM:** IT setup session (device, security training, VPN)
- **Day 2, 11:00 AM:** Manager 1:1 — role expectations
- **Day 4, 3:00 PM:** Department deep-dive session with your function's leadership
- **Day 5, 4:00 PM:** Week 1 wrap-up survey and manager check-in

## 30-60-90 Day Milestones
- **30 days:** Complete all mandatory training; have full system access; understand team's current priorities.
- **60 days:** Take ownership of at least one small deliverable independently.
- **90 days:** Formal check-in with manager and HR to review performance-so-far and set goals for the next quarter.

## Common First-Week Questions
- **"Who do I contact if my laptop isn't working?"** → Raise a ticket via NovaWorks Desk or email helpdesk@novaworks-example.com.
- **"When do I get access to Slack/email?"** → Both are provisioned before Day 1; if missing, contact IT immediately.
- **"Who is my onboarding buddy?"** → Assigned by your manager and announced during Day 1 orientation.
- **"What if I haven't met my manager yet?"** → A Day 2 1:1 is scheduled automatically; if it's missing from your calendar, contact your HR Business Partner.
