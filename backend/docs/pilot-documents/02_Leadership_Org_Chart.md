## Department Heads by Function
- **Engineering:** VP Engineering reports to CTO; team leads run Platform, AI/ML, and Infrastructure squads.
- **Product:** VP Product reports to CTO; owns roadmap for NovaWorks IQ, Desk, and Insights.
- **Finance:** FP&A and Procurement directors report to CFO.
- **People/HR:** Talent Acquisition, HR Operations, and L&D directors report to the Chief People Officer.
- **Sales:** Regional VPs (APAC, NA) and VP Customer Success report to the CRO.
- **Legal & Compliance:** General Counsel oversees compliance, contracts, and data protection.

## Board of Directors
The company maintains a 5-member board including both co-founders and two independent directors representing institutional investors. Board-level details are confidential and not exposed via the internal knowledge assistant.
