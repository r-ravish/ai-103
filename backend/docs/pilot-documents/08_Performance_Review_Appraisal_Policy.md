# Performance Review & Appraisal Policy — NovaWorks Technologies

## Document Info
- **Document ID:** POL-HR-005
- **Owner:** People Team (L&D / Performance Management)
- **Last Reviewed:** September 2026
- **Applies to:** All full-time employees who have completed probation

## 1. Review Cycle
NovaWorks runs a **biannual** performance review cycle:
- **Mid-Year Review (June):** Informal, developmental — focused on goal progress and feedback, no formal rating.
- **Annual Review (December):** Formal — includes a rating, compensation review input, and promotion consideration.

New joiners complete a **90-day probation review** before entering the standard cycle.

## 2. Review Process
1. **Self-assessment:** Employee submits a self-review in NovaWorks Desk — HR Module, covering goals achieved, challenges, and growth areas.
2. **Manager assessment:** Manager reviews self-assessment, gathers peer feedback (360-degree, optional at manager's discretion), and submits a rating.
3. **Calibration:** Department heads calibrate ratings across teams to ensure consistency before finalization.
4. **1:1 discussion:** Manager shares final rating and feedback in a dedicated 1:1 meeting — ratings are never delivered only in writing.

## 3. Rating Scale

| Rating | Label | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | Outstanding | Significantly exceeded expectations; top ~5% of performers |
| 4 | Exceeds Expectations | Consistently exceeded goals |
| 3 | Meets Expectations | Solid, reliable performance — the expected baseline |
| 2 | Needs Improvement | Falling short on one or more key goals; formal development plan required |
| 1 | Unsatisfactory | Significant performance concerns; triggers a Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) |

The majority of employees are expected to land at rating 3 in a healthy performance distribution; ratings of 4–5 are reserved for genuinely exceptional contribution.

## 4. Goal Setting
- Goals are set at the start of each half using an OKR-style framework (Objectives + 2–4 Key Results).
- Goals should be visible to the manager and, where relevant, the broader team via the shared goals tracker.
- Mid-cycle goal changes are allowed with manager agreement if business priorities shift.

## 5. Promotion Criteria
Promotion eligibility generally requires:
- A rating of 3 or above for at least the two most recent review cycles.
- Manager nomination, supported by evidence of sustained scope/impact beyond the current level.
- Department head and (for senior levels) a cross-functional promotion panel review.
- Promotions are processed twice a year, aligned with the Annual Review cycle in December and a smaller mid-year cycle in June for exceptional cases.

## 6. Performance Improvement Plans (PIP)
- Triggered by a rating of 1, or 2 for two consecutive cycles.
- A PIP is a structured 30–60-day plan with specific, measurable goals, set jointly by the manager, employee, and HR.
- Outcomes: successful completion returns the employee to standard review cycle; unsuccessful completion may lead to role reassignment or separation, per the Exit & Resignation Policy.

## Common Questions
- **"When is my next review?"** → Annual Review in December; Mid-Year Review in June.
- **"What rating do most people get?"** → Rating 3 (Meets Expectations) is the expected baseline for solid performers.
- **"How do promotions work?"** → Requires 2 consecutive cycles of rating 3+, manager nomination, and panel review; processed twice yearly.

*This document should ground process and rating-scale questions. Individual review outcomes and ratings are confidential and are never surfaced through the general knowledge assistant.*
