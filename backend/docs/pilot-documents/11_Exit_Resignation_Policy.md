# Exit & Resignation Policy — NovaWorks Technologies

## Document Info
- **Document ID:** POL-HR-008
- **Owner:** People Team (HR Operations)
- **Last Reviewed:** September 2026
- **Applies to:** All full-time employees

## 1. Resignation Process
1. Employee submits resignation in writing via NovaWorks Desk — HR Module → "Initiate Resignation," or by email to their manager and HR if system access is an issue.
2. HR confirms receipt and shares the **Last Working Day (LWD)** based on notice period rules (Section 2).
3. Manager and HR jointly plan a **knowledge transfer (KT) plan** for the notice period.
4. Exit formalities (Section 4) are completed before or on the LWD.

## 2. Notice Period

| Level | Notice Period |
|---|---|
| Individual Contributor (L1–L4) | 30 days |
| Senior IC / Team Lead (L5–L6) | 60 days |
| Manager and above | 90 days |
| Probationary employees | 15 days |

- **Buyout option:** Employees may request to buy out part or all of the remaining notice period (paying the equivalent salary) subject to manager and HR approval — not guaranteed, especially for roles with active critical projects.
- **Early release:** May be granted at the company's discretion if a smooth handover is achievable before the standard notice period ends.

## 3. Exit Interview
- Scheduled by HR during the final week of the notice period.
- Conducted by an HR Business Partner not directly involved in the employee's reporting chain, to encourage candid feedback.
- Covers: reason for leaving, manager/team feedback, role clarity, compensation perception, and suggestions for improvement.
- Feedback is aggregated anonymously into quarterly attrition insights shared with leadership; individual responses are not shared with the employee's direct manager verbatim.

## 4. Asset Return
Before the Last Working Day, employees must return:
- Company laptop, monitor, and any peripherals issued.
- Access cards / building ID badges.
- Company phone or SIM, if issued.
- Any physical documents, prototypes, or client materials.

IT deactivates all system access (email, Slack, VPN, code repos) at 11:59 PM on the Last Working Day. Asset return is tracked via NovaWorks Desk — IT Module → "Exit Checklist," and full-and-final settlement is held pending confirmation of asset return.

## 5. Full & Final (F&F) Settlement
- Processed within **30–45 days** of the Last Working Day.
- Includes: pending salary, encashable Earned Leave balance, any pending reimbursements, minus deductions (e.g., unreturned assets, notice period shortfall buyout, loan recoveries).
- F&F statement is shared via the HR portal; queries can be raised with HR Operations within 60 days of settlement.
- Relieving letter and experience certificate are issued on the Last Working Day or within 5 business days after successful asset return.

## 6. Involuntary Separation
For terminations initiated by the company (performance-based per the PIP process, or for cause), the notice period, severance, and settlement terms are governed by the specific separation agreement and applicable local labor law, which may differ from the standard resignation process above.

## Common Questions
- **"What's my notice period?"** → Depends on level: 30/60/90 days for IC/Senior/Manager+ respectively; 15 days on probation.
- **"When will I get my final settlement?"** → Within 30–45 days of your Last Working Day.
- **"What do I need to return before I leave?"** → Laptop, ID badge, phone/SIM, and any physical company property — tracked via the IT Exit Checklist.
- **"Can I leave before my notice period ends?"** → Possible via buyout or early release, subject to manager/HR approval.

*This document should ground resignation process, notice period, and asset-return questions. Specific settlement amounts are confidential and should be routed to a personal HR portal lookup, not answered generically.*
