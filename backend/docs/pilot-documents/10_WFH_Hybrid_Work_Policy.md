# Work From Home / Hybrid Work Policy — NovaWorks Technologies

## Document Info
- **Document ID:** POL-HR-007
- **Owner:** People Team / IT
- **Last Reviewed:** September 2026
- **Applies to:** All employees in eligible roles

## 1. Eligibility
- **Hybrid-eligible roles:** Most Engineering, Product, Marketing, Finance, and HR roles.
- **Office-required roles:** Front-desk/facilities, on-site IT support, and roles explicitly designated "on-site" in the offer letter.
- **Fully remote roles:** Available for specific roles explicitly hired as remote (noted in offer letter); subject to manager and department head approval.
- New hires must complete their **first 30 days** primarily in-office (or with daily video presence if hired remote) before shifting to their standard hybrid pattern.

## 2. Standard Hybrid Pattern
- **Default:** 3 days in-office, 2 days work-from-home per week, decided at the team level by the department head.
- Teams publish their designated in-office days (e.g., "Engineering: Tue/Wed/Thu in-office") so collaboration days are predictable.
- Employees may swap an in-office day for a WFH day with manager approval for personal circumstances, provided the weekly ratio is maintained.

## 3. Requesting Additional WFH Days
- **Occasional (1–2 days):** Inform your manager via Slack/email; no formal approval workflow required.
- **Extended (a week or more, e.g., travel or personal reasons):** Submit a request via NovaWorks Desk — HR Module, approved by manager and HR.
- **Fully remote for a defined period (e.g., relocating temporarily):** Requires Chief People Officer approval and an updated data-security acknowledgment if working from outside the home country.

## 4. Equipment Provided
| Item | Provided For |
|---|---|
| Laptop | All employees |
| Monitor, keyboard, mouse | Office desk setup; remote hires can request a one-time home setup kit |
| Headset | Roles requiring frequent calls (Sales, Support, CS) |
| Internet/phone allowance | Remote and hybrid employees, per Compensation & Benefits Overview |
| Ergonomic chair | On request, subject to manager approval and budget, for confirmed WFH arrangements |

Equipment requests are raised via NovaWorks Desk — IT Module → "Equipment Request."

## 5. WFH Expectations
- Maintain the same core collaboration hours (11:00 AM–4:00 PM local time) as in-office employees.
- Ensure a stable internet connection and a quiet, professional environment for calls.
- Remain reachable on Slack/email during working hours; unexplained unavailability is treated the same as an office no-show.
- Company data must only be accessed over the approved VPN (see Information Security & Data Protection Policy); public Wi-Fi without VPN is prohibited.
- WFH does not change deliverable deadlines or meeting attendance expectations.

## 6. Manager Responsibilities
- Publish and enforce the team's designated in-office days.
- Ensure hybrid arrangements don't disadvantage remote team members in visibility, opportunities, or meeting scheduling (default to hybrid-friendly meeting formats).

## Common Questions
- **"What's our WFH policy?"** → Default 3 days in-office, 2 days WFH per week, per team-designated schedule.
- **"Can I work from home permanently?"** → Only for roles explicitly hired as fully remote, or with CPO-approved exception.
- **"What equipment will the company give me for WFH?"** → Laptop is standard; monitor/chair/headset available on request depending on role.
