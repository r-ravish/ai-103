# Work From Home Policy

Eligible employees may work from home for up to 3 days per week, subject to team requirements and manager approval.

Employees working remotely must remain available during their team's designated working hours and must attend required meetings.

## Equipment

The company may provide approved laptops, monitors, keyboards, and other equipment required for remote work. Employees are responsible for protecting company equipment from unauthorized access.

## Working Hours

Employees must maintain their normal working schedule while working remotely unless an alternative schedule has been approved by their manager.

## Office Attendance

Employees may be required to attend the office for team meetings, training, planning sessions, security activities, or other business requirements.
