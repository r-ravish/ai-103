# Employee Expense Reimbursement Policy

Employees may request reimbursement for reasonable business expenses incurred while performing authorized company activities.

All reimbursement requests must be submitted through the expense management system within 30 days of the expense date.

## Required Documentation

Employees must provide an itemized receipt for expenses above INR 1,000. The receipt should clearly show the date, merchant, and amount.

## Travel Expenses

Approved business travel may be reimbursed for transportation, accommodation, and eligible meals according to company limits.

Employees should obtain manager approval before booking travel unless the travel is required for an urgent business purpose.

## Approval

Expense claims are reviewed by the employee's manager. Finance may request additional documentation before approving reimbursement.
