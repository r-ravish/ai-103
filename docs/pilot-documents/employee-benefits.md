# Employee Benefits Guide

Eligible full-time employees receive access to the company's standard benefits program.

## Health Insurance

Employees may enroll in the company health insurance plan according to the enrollment rules applicable to their employment category.

Eligible dependents can be added during the designated enrollment period or following a qualifying life event.

## Retirement Benefits

Employees may participate in the company's retirement savings program after satisfying the applicable eligibility requirements.

## Learning and Development

Employees may request reimbursement for approved professional courses, certifications, conferences, and training programs related to their role.

Learning requests may require manager approval and must comply with the annual learning budget.
